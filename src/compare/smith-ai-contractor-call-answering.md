## The short version

**Pick Smith.ai** if you want live human receptionists, bilingual English and Spanish coverage, or a general answering service you set up yourself, and your call volume is modest.

**Pick Couch & Craft** if you run an HVAC, plumbing, electrical or roofing shop and want someone else to build, run and tune lead response for your trade, so jobs land on your calendar.

Smith.ai bills its human receptionist plans per call, not per minute. Its published entry plan is $300 a month for 30 calls, plus $11.50 for each extra call. Here's the math on that plan. Plans change, so check smith.ai/pricing.

| --- | --- |
| 30 | $300 |
| 60 | about $645 |
| 100 | about $1,105 (a higher tier may cost less) |

## Where Smith.ai is the better fit

- You want a live person to answer every call, or a mix of AI and people.
- You need bilingual English and Spanish answering.
- You want recordings, transcripts and summaries, plus tools like Calendly, Acuity or Zapier.
- Your volume is low enough that a small per-call plan costs less than a monthly fee.

## Where Couch & Craft is the better fit

- **Built for your trade.** Emergency or routine, inside your service area or not, and the job-type and urgency questions that matter for HVAC, plumbing, electrical and roofing.
- **Done for you.** I set it up, watch it and tune it. You don't learn another tool.
- **Booked jobs, not call counts.** Reports show appointments on your calendar, and missed-call text-back and follow-up are part of it, because answering is only half the job.

## How setup works

1. You fill out a short intake form: trades, service area, hours and calendar.
2. We get on a kickoff call to confirm job types and emergency rules.
3. I set up the text-back, the questions and the calendar booking.
4. I watch real conversations and adjust the rules.
5. You see booked jobs in a regular report.

## Questions

### Smith.ai has real people. Do you?

No. Smith.ai's live and hybrid options are a real strength. Lead Response is managed AI that I set up, run and tune. Ask me on a call exactly how emergencies and handoffs to you would work before you buy.

### Smith.ai's AI plan is cheap. Why pay more?

The low-cost AI plan covers a small number of calls. Human and hybrid plans are billed per call with overage, so the bill grows with your call volume. Run the table above with your own call count.

### I already use Jobber or Housecall Pro. Does that matter?

Bookings land on your calendar. Tell me which calendar and tools you use and I'll confirm what connects before you commit.

### Will it sound robotic or book bad jobs?

That's what setup and tuning are for. I write the qualification rules for your trade (job type, urgency, service area), read real conversations and adjust over time.

### Can I buy the scripts and do it myself?

Yes. The [shop](/shop/) has the Emergency Call Triage Scripts Pack, the Speed-to-Lead Templates and the AI Prompt Library. Done-for-you is for owners who'd rather not build it.
